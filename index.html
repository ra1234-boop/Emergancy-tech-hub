ABOUT THE PLATFORM

Knowledge can make a difference.

Emergency Tech Hub is an independent information and awareness platform with a South African focus. It explores how emergency services work, how technology supports them, and how members of the public can become better prepared.

The aim is to share clear, practical and responsible information in a way that is accessible to everyone.

  • Emergency services and their roles
  • Communication systems and emergency technology
  • First-aid awareness and personal preparedness
  • Public safety information and verified updates

SOUTH AFRICAN QUICK REFERENCE

Emergency numbers.

Save the relevant contact numbers for your area. Tap a number on a mobile phone to call.

Cellphone emergencies112

Emergency call number from a cellphone.

Police emergencies10111

South African Police Service emergency line.

Ambulance10177

Ambulance emergency services.

Important: Emergency Tech Hub is an independent information platform. It is not an emergency call centre and cannot dispatch assistance. In a real emergency, contact the relevant emergency service directly. Numbers and availability can vary by area and network; check local official sources. For Cape Town, the City also lists 107 from a landline and 021 480 7700 for emergencies.

When calling, clearly give the location, explain what happened, provide a callback number if requested, and follow the call-taker's instructions.
